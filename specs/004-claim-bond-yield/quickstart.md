# Quickstart: Claim Bond Yield Button

## Prerequisites

- Wallet connected and on correct network
- Project has claimable yield

## Steps

1. Open Dashboard and locate a bond card with yield > 0.
2. Click Claim button beside Sell.
3. Confirm transaction in wallet.
4. Observe pending → confirming → success toast.
5. Verify bond yield and totals update automatically.

## Notes

- If button is disabled, hover for hint (no yield or wrong network).
- Errors present actionable messages; retry once network/state is correct.
