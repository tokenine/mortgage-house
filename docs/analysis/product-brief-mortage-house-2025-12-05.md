## Executive Summary

**mortage-house** transforms traditional mortgage funding into an accessible, liquid, and automated investment system. By creating one smart contract per property, the platform enables fractional mortgage funding starting from 1 USDT, with automated pro-rata distribution of principal and interest payments. Unlike traditional mortgage markets that exclude retail investors and rely on manual accounting, mortage-house provides complete on-chain transparency, instant secondary market liquidity, and mathematically fair distribution while maintaining regulatory compliance through off-chain underwriting and legal frameworks.

---

## Core Vision

### Problem Statement

Traditional mortgage funding suffers from three critical failures: exclusion (only banks and institutions can participate), opacity (manual accounting creates uncertainty about ownership and payments), and illiquidity (investors are locked in until maturity). Small investors cannot access stable, asset-backed mortgage opportunities, while borrowers face slow, bureaucratic funding processes that limit their access to capital.

### Problem Impact

The current system locks away trillions in stable, asset-backed investment opportunities from retail investors, creates operational bottlenecks for mortgage providers through manual reconciliation, and delays funding for borrowers who need quick access to capital. This inefficiency costs the market billions in lost productivity and creates artificial barriers to entry that serve no one except legacy financial institutions.

### Why Existing Solutions Fall Short

Current solutions either replicate traditional finance's manual inefficiencies (mortgage REITs, private lending) or focus on different asset classes entirely (DeFi platforms). No existing solution combines fractional access, automated distribution, and secondary market liquidity for mortgage-backed investments. Real estate tokenization platforms focus on property ownership rather than the mortgage layer, leaving the funding problem unsolved.

### Proposed Solution

mortage-house creates a "mini REIT" for each property through a dedicated smart contract that manages fractional ownership and automated distribution. Investors buy shares starting from 1 USDT, participate in pro-rata distribution of principal and interest payments, and can trade their shares anytime in a built-in marketplace. Each contract operates as an isolated, transparent settlement engine while the master wallet handles compliance, credit underwriting, and legal enforcement off-chain.

### Key Differentiators

1. **One Contract Per Property Architecture**: Complete isolation and transparency, each mortgage operates independently with zero risk of cross-contamination.

2. **Automated Pro-Rata Distribution**: Mathematical fairness guaranteed by smart contracts, eliminating manual calculations and reconciliation errors.

3. **Integrated Secondary Market**: Immediate liquidity for traditionally illiquid assets, enabling investors to trade positions anytime.

4. **Regulatory-Compliant Hybrid Model**: Off-chain legal frameworks and underwriting combined with on-chain distribution automation, creating the best of both worlds.

5. **Democratized Access**: 1 USDT minimum investment opens mortgage markets to retail investors while maintaining professional-grade risk management.

## MVP Scope

### Core Features

#### **Smart Contract Core (1 Complete Mortgage Lifecycle)**

**Deployment Phase:**
- Deploy new mortgage contract for single property
- Configure: loanAmount, masterWallet (borrower), USDT token address
- Initialize contract state and ownership

**Funding Phase:**
- `invest(uint256 amount)` - Investors deposit USDT
- 1 share = 1 USDT unit issued to investors
- Track `shares[address]` and `totalFunded` in real-time
- Transfer shares from masterWallet to investor

**Loan Disbursement:**
- `withdrawLoan(uint256 amount)` - Master wallet withdraws funded capital
- Enables real loan simulation for borrowers
- Prevents withdrawal beyond total funded amount

**Repayment Processing:**
- `depositPrincipal(uint256 amount)` - Track principal repayments
- `depositInterest(uint256 amount)` - Track interest payments
- Update `principalRepaid` and `interestPaid` counters
- All business logic (schedules, credit rules) handled off-chain

**Investor Payouts:**
- `withdraw()` - Calculate and transfer pro-rata distributions
- Automatically compute withdrawable principal + interest per investor
- Track `withdrawnPrincipal[user]` and `withdrawnInterest[user]`
- Support partial repayments over time with accurate calculations

**Contract Control:**
- `setStage(FUNDING | ACTIVE | CLOSED)` - Master wallet stage management
- UI behavior adaptation based on contract stage
- Stage transitions don't restrict core flows (as per your requirement)

**Event System:**
- `Invested(investor, amount, shares)` - Funding events
- `LoanWithdrawn(amount)` - Disbursement events
- `PrincipalDeposited(amount)` / `InterestDeposited(amount)` - Repayment events
- `Withdrawn(user, principal, interest)` - Investor payout events
- `StageChanged(oldStage, newStage)` - Status change events

#### **Frontend Interface (Essential User Interactions)**

**Investor Dashboard:**
- Wallet connection and balance display
- Real-time view of: loan amount, total funded, personal shares, withdrawable amounts
- One-click investment and withdrawal buttons
- Clear visualization of investment status and earnings

**Master Wallet Operator Panel:**
- Mortgage contract status overview
- Loan withdrawal, principal deposit, interest deposit controls
- Stage management interface (Funding → Active → Closed)
- Simplified operator workflow replacing spreadsheet management

**Operator Dashboard:**
- Key metrics display: total invested, principal repaid, interest repaid, total withdrawn
- Active contract stage indicator
- Real-time funding progress and repayment tracking
